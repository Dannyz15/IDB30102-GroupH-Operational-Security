# 03_Architecure

This section will explore the kind of architecure this research proposal has created. The architecture works in stages. 

1. Take in the secondary evidences (40 peer reviewed studies)
2. Split into two. Technical indicator and human-factor indicator. Technical indicator is based on existing framework such as CVSS, attack-graph, etc. Human-factor indicator is based on security-awareness scores, stress indicator, etc.
3. Normalisation and weighting engine of technical and human-factor indicator
4. Combining both indicators into a singular Integrated Operational Risk Score (IORS)
5. *IORS*  is then compared with other existing module such as NIST CSF or OCTAVE Allegro as benchmarking
6. Risk prioritisation output and recommendation
